OKYIN

Messages that meet you where you are. WhatsApp and SMS, adapted for Blind, Deaf and Cognitive users, in Twi and English — on the phone, offline.

Download OKYIN Any Android phone, 7 and up · 163 MB

Your phone will warn about installing outside the Play Store. Choose Install anyway. If Play Protect blocks it, open Play Store › your picture › Play Protect › settings, and switch off scanning while you install.

Switch these on once

  1. Open OKYIN and pick a profile.
  2. Notification access — Settings › Notifications › Special access › Notification access › OKYIN message reader. Without it no message reaches OKYIN.
  3. OKYIN helper — Settings › Accessibility › Installed apps. Opens OKYIN from a held volume key, and presses Send in WhatsApp for you.
  4. Contacts and SMS — OKYIN asks the first time you send something.
  5. All files access — only if you want WhatsApp voice notes played aloud. WhatsApp hides them from every other route.

The voices download themselves the first time you read a message. Use Wi-Fi for that once.

How each person uses it

Blind or low vision

Two taps anywhere open a black board: write a message with your finger, one character at a time. Two taps is a space, swipe left rubs out, hold still finishes. OKYIN reads it back, then asks for the number with wh or sms on the end.

Three taps dictate the message instead, for anyone who does not write letters. Hold a volume key for three seconds to open OKYIN from any screen.

Deaf or hard of hearing

Every message arrives on screen and is signed by the avatar, one message after another, with a vibration for each.

Cognitive or developmental

Messages are broken into one idea per line, with what to do first, the key facts, and pictures. Tap to hear any line.

Everyone

Communicate on the home screen writes a message and sends it by WhatsApp or SMS to a saved contact.
